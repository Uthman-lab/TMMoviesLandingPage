Discover movies with TMMovies!

Immerse yourself in the world of cinema with TMMovies, a beautifully designed app powered by TMDB APIs. Whether you're a casual movie-goer or a cinephile, TMMovies is the perfect app to keep you informed, entertained, and connected to the movies you love.

About

The Movie App provides users with an engaging and intuitive way to explore movies from the TMDB database. With features like browsing trending and top-rated movies, searching for specific titles, managing a personalized wish list, viewing detailed information of movies, watching trailers, and tracking their desired films. The app ensures a seamless and personalized experience.

Features

Trending Movies

Displays a list of currently trending movies based on TMDB data.

Upcoming Movies

Shows a list of movies scheduled for release in the near future

Top Rated Movies

Displays a list of movies with the highest ratings

Now Playing Movies

Shows movies currently playing in theaters

Search Functionality

Allows users to search for movies by title, keywords, etc.

Movie Details

For each movie, users can access detailed information including: Trailers, Plot, Cast & Crew, Reviews etc

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TMMovies?

TMMovies is your ultimate movie companion. It lets you explore trending, popular, and upcoming films, view detailed movie information, manage your wish list, and more.

TMMovies free to use?

Yes, TMMovies is free to download and use. Some features may require an internet connection.

Where does TMMovies get its movie data?

TMMovies is powered by TMDB (The Movie Database) APIs, a reliable and popular source for movie data.

Why can’t I see any movies?

Ensure you have a stable internet connection. If the problem persists, try restarting the app.

The app crashed. What should I do?

Please close and reopen the app. If the issue continues, contact support via the Support URL.

Why are some movie details missing?

TMMovies relies on TMDB for data. Occasionally, some movies may not have complete information available.
